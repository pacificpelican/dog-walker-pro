Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Short version: Dog Walker Pro collects as little as possible. You can play without an account.

What is stored

  • Without an account: nothing is sent to the server for saving.
  • With an account: your screen name, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), and your saved game (points, stats, hats, and similar progress). No email address is collected.
  • In your browser: a login token is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Logging out removes it. The game does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

NPC chat

When you talk to a character, your message and some game context (such as the character's persona) are sent to the server, which forwards them to the configured AI model server (Ollama by default, often running on the same machine). If that server is unreachable, scripted replies are used instead. Please don't type personal information into the chat.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared for advertising. Standard server logs (such as IP address and requested pages) may be kept by the hosting provider for security and operations.

Your choices

  • Play without an account.
  • Log out to clear the login token from your browser.
  • Ask for your account and saved data to be deleted via the contact below.

Children

The game is family-friendly, but it is not designed to collect personal information from children. Children should have a parent or guardian's permission before creating an account.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date above shows the latest revision.
